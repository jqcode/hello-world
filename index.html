About

A life in sound

Born in London in 1960, Andy Quin is one of the world's most prolific and versatile production music composers — and a celebrated jazz pianist and theatre organist.

Not from a musical family, Andy began playing piano at the age of four. By his early teens a passion for jazz, composition and recording had taken hold, and he turned down a scholarship to the Royal College of Music to pursue a broader musical path, graduating instead from Keele University with a degree in Music and Electronics.

In 1984 James de Wolfe commissioned his debut album Mirage, which was re-released the following year as DWCD 0001 — the very first CD issued by the world's first music library. More than 80 albums for De Wolfe Music have followed, with his music heard in tens of thousands of broadcasts every year across all continents.

Away from the studio, Andy is a sought-after jazz pianist and theatre organist, regularly accompanying silent films on some of Europe's finest Wurlitzer organs.
