About

A life in sound

James Holloway is an award-winning composer whose work has been performed across Europe and North America.

Drawing on a lifelong fascination with the intersection of acoustic and electronic sound, James creates music that is both deeply personal and universally resonant. His compositions range from intimate solo pieces to large-scale orchestral works.

He studied at the Royal College of Music and has worked with ensembles including the London Symphony Orchestra, the Kronos Quartet, and the BBC Concert Orchestra. His scores are published by Edition Peters.

Selected works

Compositions

  1. 01

    Stillwater

    A single-movement work exploring stillness and gradual transformation, premiered at the Wigmore Hall, London.

    String Quartet 2024
  2. 02

    Fracture Lines

    Commissioned by the BBC Proms. A large-scale work in three movements responding to tectonic and social rupture.

    Orchestra & Electronics 2022
  3. 03

    Seven Silences

    Seven miniatures, each exploring a different quality of silence — written during lockdown and recorded at Abbey Road.

    Solo Piano 2020
  4. 04

    The Cartographer's Dream

    A song cycle setting texts by contemporary poets, weaving cartographic imagery with themes of belonging and loss.

    Choir & Chamber Orchestra 2018
  5. 05

    Drift

    A fixed-media piece constructed from field recordings gathered across the Arctic. Winner of the Prix Ars Electronica.

    Electroacoustic 2015
  6. 06

    Heliograph

    A four-movement symphony inspired by the science and mythology of sunlight, dedicated to his teacher.

    Symphony Orchestra 2012
